# Analytical Note: Monetization and In-Game Activity Research in "Secrets of Darkwood"

##  Key Findings & Product Insights

### Monetization and Player Behavior Across Races
* **The overall conversion rate into paying players** stands at **17.69%**, remaining stable within a narrow range across all character races (ranging from **17.07%** for `Elf` to **19.37%** for `Demon`).
* *Product Insight:* Baseline willingness to donate is distributed evenly across races, with no severe upward or downward skews at the top-of-funnel conversion. The `Demon` race shows a slightly higher propensity to pay, while the `Elf` race shows the lowest.

### Transaction Distribution and Pricing Structure
* Analysis of the dataset (**1,307,678 purchases** totaling 686.6 million "paradise petals") revealed a pronounced right-skewness: with an average check of **525.7** petals, the median value is only **74.86**. This indicates that the core of transactions consists of mass micro-purchases, while rare large checks (peaking at **486,615** petals) significantly pull the arithmetic mean upward. A high standard deviation (**2,517.3**) further confirms the data heterogeneity.
* **Anomalies:** **907 zero-value transactions** (0.07% of the total volume) were recorded. All of them are exclusively associated with the item *Book of Legends*. Although the share of anomalies is statistically negligible, they were excluded from calculations to avoid distorting actual consumption metrics.
